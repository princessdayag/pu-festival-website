About the Event

The PU Festival and Student Day is a once-a-year celebration at Panpacific University that brings together students, instructors, and staff for a day of fun, unity, and entertainment. It is usually held around the months of September and October, providing everyone an opportunity to relax and celebrate after months of hard work. Every year, the event features performances from talented students and organizations, along with a special guest artist to highlight the night. For PU Festival 2025, the special guest performer was the renowned Filipino rapper and songwriter GLOC-9, who brought energy and inspiration to the crowd with his powerful music and lyrics.
